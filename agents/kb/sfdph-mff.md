# SF Public Health — Mobile Food Facility Health Permit

SFDPH Environmental Health issues the **health permit** for a mobile food facility and reviews
food-safety compliance. Required for **all** vendor types that handle food, but the *depth* of
review scales with risk:

- **Cooking / hot-holding on board** (truck, trailer, pushcart_cooking) → **plan review** of the
  unit, handwashing/warewashing sinks, hot/cold holding, and a **commissary** agreement (a fixed
  base for cleaning, restocking, and wastewater disposal).
- **No-cook / prepackaged only** (pushcart_nocook) → a **lower-tier** health permit; still
  requires a commissary/base and safe handling, but no cook-line plan check for open cooking.

## What the health permit covers (`cite: sfdph-mff`)

1. **Application + plan review** of the MFF unit (scaled by cooking vs no-cook).
2. **Commissary / base of operations** agreement (cleaning, water, waste).
3. **Food safety certification** for the operator (manager/handler card).
4. **Health inspection** of the unit before operating.

These steps enter each vendor-type checklist under the **Public Health** agency, ordered before
the Public Works location application (health clearance is typically a prerequisite document
inside the **90-day window**).
