# SF Public Works — Mobile Food Facility Permit Process

Public Works issues the **Mobile Food Facility (MFF) permit** that authorizes operating on the
public right-of-way. This is the anchor permit in every vendor's checklist. The **hidden
deadline clocks** below are the ones vendors most often miss — surface them on every checklist.

## The hidden clocks (task-pinned — authoritative, `cite: sfpw-mff`)

| Clock | What it means | Why it bites |
|---|---|---|
| **30-day public notice** | After you apply for a specific location, Public Works posts a **30-day public notice**; nearby businesses/residents can object and a hearing may be set. | You cannot operate at that spot until the notice period (and any hearing) resolves. Plan ~30+ days of lead time per location. |
| **90-day document window** | Supporting documents (health, fire, insurance, business registration, etc.) must be **current/submitted within a 90-day window** for the application to stay valid. | Documents collected too early can go stale and force re-submission; too late and the application stalls. |
| **15-day appeal window** | After a decision (grant/deny) there is a **15-day window** to appeal. | Both the applicant and objectors are bound by it; a spot isn't truly settled until it passes. |

## Ordered process (structural — authoritative flow; exact form names/§ = SOURCE-NEEDED)

1. **Prepare prerequisites** — business registration (`ttx-cert`), health permit / plan review
   (`sfdph-mff`), and (if cooking with flame/LPG) a fire permit (`sffd-permit`). Keep them
   inside the **90-day document window**.
2. **Apply for the MFF permit** at Public Works for a **specific location** (and schedule for
   trucks/trailers), paying the application fee (`sfpw-fees`).
3. **30-day public notice** is posted for the location.
4. **Hearing (if triggered)** by objections.
5. **Decision** — grant or deny.
6. **15-day appeal window** runs.
7. **Permit issued** — pay issuance/annual fee (`sfpw-fees`); begin operating within the
   permitted location, schedule, and clearance rules (`dpw-182101`).

## Fees (`source: sfpw-fees`)

Public Works charges an **application fee** and an **annual permit/issuance fee** for an MFF, and
may charge a separate posting/notice fee. Exact dollar amounts: see `clearance-rules.md`
