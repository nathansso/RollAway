# SF Treasurer & Tax Collector — Business Registration

Every vendor operating as a business in San Francisco must hold a **Business Registration
Certificate** from the Treasurer & Tax Collector. This is required for **all** vendor types
(`truck`, `trailer`, `pushcart_cooking`, `pushcart_nocook`) — it is not conditional on cooking or
on being a motor vehicle.

## What it covers (`cite: ttx-cert`)

1. **Register the business** with the Treasurer & Tax Collector.
2. **Pay the registration fee** (scaled by estimated gross receipts).
3. **Keep the certificate current** (annual renewal) and inside the **90-day document window**
   when applying for the Public Works MFF permit.

This step enters every vendor-type checklist under the **Treasurer** agency, ordered early
(it is a prerequisite document for the Public Works application).
